Sarkari Form Photo & Signature Size Guide (India)

Every application cycle, candidates get stuck at the upload step because their photo or signature doesn't match the official spec. Portals check three things — file size in KB, dimensions in pixels, and format (JPG/JPEG). The good news: every fix is a 2-minute job with free tools, and your files never leave your device.

Important: boards revise specs in each year's notification. The specs below are pinned to the notifications named in each row — always cross-check against your current notification before uploading.

Master spec table: photo, signature & document sizes

ExamPhotoSignatureSource

SSC CHSL (uploads)JPG, 20–50 KB, ~3.5×4.5 cmJPG, 10–20 KBCHSL 2025 notice

* The CGL 2025 notice says ~6.0×2.0 cm in para 9.6 but ~4.0×2.0 cm in its annexure — confirm the figure inside your live application module.

Fix any photo or signature in 2 minutes

  1. Get the KB right. Open Compress Image, upload your file, and set the exact target size (e.g. 20 KB). The target-size mode binary-searches the compression level until your file lands at or just under your number — no guessing quality percentages.
  2. Get the dimensions right. Open Resize Image and enter the exact pixel values from the table above (the tool takes pixel inputs).
  3. Get the format right. If the portal wants JPG but your file is PNG, run it through Convert Image (JPG/PNG/WebP/BMP/GIF/SVG in → JPEG, PNG or WebP out).
  4. Crop and clean. Trim borders with Crop Image. For signatures, Signature Maker produces a clean transparent PNG (450×200 px). For ID-style photos, Passport Photo formats standard presets.
  5. Pre-upload checklist. File opens fine? Size inside the KB range? Pixels match? Format JPG/JPEG? Background plain, face clearly visible? If yes — upload.
