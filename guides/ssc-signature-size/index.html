SSC Signature Size: 10–20 KB Exact Specs

The Staff Selection Commission is strict about signature uploads: wrong file size and the portal simply refuses your form. Here are the exact specs from the official notice, plus a 2-minute method to make any signature fit — free, no signup, files never leave your device.

SSC signature spec (CGL 2025 notice, para 9.6)

RequirementSpec
FormatJPG / JPEG
File size10–20 KB
Dimensions~6.0 cm × 2.0 cm*
StyleRunning-hand signature, dark ink on white paper — no block letters
* The same notice's annexure says ~4.0×2.0 cm. Confirm the figure inside your live application module before uploading.
Live photo capture: SSC CGL uses live photo capture at the application stage (policy since the CGL 2025 notice, confirmed in the 2026 notice) — but the signature is still uploaded as a scanned file, and this guide covers that upload. Always verify against the current official notification; specs change year to year.

Why signatures get rejected

The three classic reasons: (1) file bigger than 20 KB — the portal's uploader simply refuses it; (2) block letters or printed name instead of a running-hand signature; (3) grey/dirty background from a bad scan or shadowy phone photo. All three are fixable below.

Make an SSC-ready signature in 2 minutes

  1. Sign it properly. On plain white paper, sign in running hand with a black or dark-blue pen. Keep it compact.
  2. Digitize it. Scan at 200 DPI, or photograph it in bright, even light (no shadows, no flash glare).
  3. Crop tight. Open Crop Image and trim all white margins so the signature fills the frame. This alone often drops the file size dramatically.
  4. Hit 10–20 KB. Open Compress Image, upload the cropped signature, and set the target to 15 KB (middle of the range).
  5. Fix the format if needed. PNG file? Run it through Convert Image to get JPG. Prefer a clean digital signature? Use Signature Maker (transparent PNG, 450×200 px), then compress to 15 KB the same way.

Pre-upload checklist

  • File is JPG/JPEG
  • Size between 10 and 20 KB (aim 15 KB)
  • Running hand, dark ink, white background
  • No shadows, borders, or extra paper visible
  • Opens correctly in the portal preview
