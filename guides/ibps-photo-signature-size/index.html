IBPS Photo & Signature Size Guide (PO, Clerk, RRB)

IBPS (PO, Clerk, RRB) asks for four separate uploads. Missing any one blocks your application. Below are the exact specs from the official IBPS scanning guidelines, plus free steps to make each file fit — no signup, files never leave your device.

Specs from the official IBPS “Guidelines for Scanning & Uploading of Photograph & Signature” (ibpsonline.ibps.in), consistent with the IBPS PO/MT-XVI notification of 30 June 2026. IBPS revises its notification yearly — verify each number against your current notification before uploading.

IBPS upload specs

UploadFormatSizeDimensions
PhotographJPG20–50 KB4.5 × 3.5 cm (200 × 230 px preferred)
SignatureJPG10–20 KB140 × 60 px
Left thumb impressionJPG20–50 KB240 × 240 px
Handwritten declarationJPG50–100 KB800 × 400 px

Resize each upload with DocToolKit (free, no signup)

  1. Photograph (20–50 KB): recent colour photo, light background. Resize Image to 200 × 230 px, then Compress Image with a 35 KB target (middle of the band).
  2. Signature (10–20 KB): sign on white paper with dark ink, crop tight, compress to a 15 KB target. Or generate one with Signature Maker and compress the same way.
  3. Thumb impression (20–50 KB): ink your left thumb, press on white paper, photograph in good light, crop tight, resize to 240 × 240 px, compress to a 35 KB target.
  4. Handwritten declaration (50–100 KB): write the declaration text exactly as worded in the notification (IBPS rejects paraphrased text), photograph clearly, compress to a 75 KB target.
  5. Format fixes: if any file is PNG or another format, run it through Convert Image to get JPG first. (Note: HEIC files aren't supported — convert them on your phone first.)

Pre-upload checklist

  • All four files are JPG
  • Photo 20–50 KB · Signature 10–20 KB · Thumb 20–50 KB · Declaration 50–100 KB
  • Declaration text matches the notification word-for-word
  • Photo is recent (IBPS asks for a recent photograph)
  • Each file previews correctly in the portal
