Passport Photo Maker India — 3.5×4.5 cm Guide

Indian passport photos follow ICAO-aligned specs: exact size, plain white background, and your face filling most of the frame. Make one free in minutes — no signup, your photo never leaves your device.

Indian passport photo specs (official)

RequirementSpec
Size4.5 cm (length) × 3.5 cm (width)
Digital size630 × 810 px
BackgroundPlain white, unaltered
Face80–85% of the photo, fully visible, neutral expression, mouth closed
FormatJPG / JPEG
Passport Seva Kendra note: PSKs capture your photo live — you don't upload one there. These specs apply to DPC/SPC/CSC submissions and to visa/ID uses. Visa photos vary by country (US: 2×2 inch; Schengen: 3.5×4.5 cm) — always check the specific embassy's spec.

Make it with DocToolKit's Passport Photo tool

  1. Take the photo. Stand against a plain white wall in daylight. Phone at eye level, 1–1.5 m away. No shadows on the face or background, no glasses glare, neutral expression, mouth closed.
  2. Format it. Open Passport Photo and upload your photo. Pick the India 35×45 mm preset (outputs JPEG on a white background).
  3. Fix size and format. For exact KB or pixel needs, follow up with Resize Image and Compress Image. Wrong format? Convert Image handles JPG/PNG conversion.
  4. Print-ready. Keep the high-resolution version and print at 300 DPI — a 3.5×4.5 cm photo at 300 DPI is roughly 413×531 px. Most studio printers accept the JPG directly from a USB drive. (Note: set 300 DPI in your print dialog — image files don't carry a DPI tag by themselves.)

Common rejection reasons (and fixes)

  • Shadows on background → retake in even daylight; never use flash against a wall.
  • Face too small → face must fill 80–85% of the frame; crop with the Crop Image tool if needed.
  • Smiling / teeth visible → neutral expression, mouth closed.
  • Old photo → use a recent photo (taken within the last 6 months for passport applications).

FAQs

4.5 cm (length) × 3.5 cm (width), plain white background, face covering 80–85% of the frame, 630×810 px digital size.
