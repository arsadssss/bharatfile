UPSC & RRB Photo/Signature Upload Specs

UPSC is generous on file sizes but strict on format; RRB has moved to live photo capture with a tight signature spec. Below are the current official specs — pinned to the named notifications — plus free steps to make each file fit.

UPSC CSE upload specs (per UPSC FAQ-Examination, updated 09/07/2024)

UploadFormatSizeDimensions
PhotographJPG20–300 KB350×350 – 1000×1000 px
SignatureJPG20–300 KB350×350 – 550×1000 px
Photo ID (Aadhaar / PAN / Passport / Voter ID)PDFUp to 300 KB—
UPSC requires a recent photograph — official wording: not more than 10 days old from the start of the online application process. The photo clause changes by year — read your notification's exact wording.

RRB upload specs (per CEN 06/2026 — NTPC Graduate, Oct 2026)

UploadFormatSizeDimensions
PhotographLive capture — no file uploaded. Head should cover ≥50% of the frame; photos-of-photos risk summary rejection.
SignatureJPG / JPEG30–49 KB≥140×60 px, 35×20 mm scan box, black ink, ≥100 DPI
Category certificate (SC/ST/OBC/EWS)JPG / PDFPer notification

Make each file in 2 minutes with DocToolKit

  1. UPSC photo: recent colour photo, white background. Resize Image to ~500×700 px, then Compress Image with a 100 KB target (comfortably inside 20–300 KB with great quality).
  2. UPSC signature: sign on white paper, crop tight, compress to 50 KB.
  3. UPSC Photo ID PDF: scan your ID and use an image-to-PDF step; keep it under 300 KB.
  4. RRB signature: sign inside a 35×20 mm box with black ink, scan at ≥100 DPI, crop tight, compress to a 35 KB target (middle of 30–49 KB).
  5. Format fixes: PNG or other formats → Convert Image → JPG before uploading. (HEIC not supported — convert on your phone first.)

Pre-upload checklist

  • UPSC: photo 20–300 KB JPG · signature 20–300 KB JPG · ID PDF ≤ 300 KB
  • RRB: signature 30–49 KB JPG, ≥140×60 px
  • Photo is recent (UPSC: per the notification's recency clause)
  • All files preview correctly in the portal
