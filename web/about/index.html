About DocToolKit

DocToolKit was created to solve a critical privacy flaw in modern web utilities: the requirement to upload sensitive personal, financial, and legal documents to remote cloud servers just to perform basic file operations.

1. Our Core Mission

Our mission is to provide powerful, professional document and image utilities that run entirely on your own device. Whether you are a student submitting exam applications, a professional managing confidential contracts, or an applicant compressing identity proofs for government portals, your paperwork should remain strictly private.

2. 100% On-Device Architecture

Traditional PDF compressors and image converters receive your file, process it on an unknown remote server, and send it back. DocToolKit takes a radically different approach:

  • Local WebAssembly: PDF parsing, rasterization, and compression execute inside your browser via compiled WebAssembly and native PDF.js engines.
  • HTML5 Canvas: Image compression, format conversion (JPG, PNG, WebP), resizing, rotation, and photo cropping happen directly on your graphics hardware using HTML5 Canvas.
  • Zero Server Transmission: Your confidential file bytes never cross the network. Even if your internet connection drops midway through processing, the tools continue working smoothly.

3. Progressive Web App (PWA) Capability

DocToolKit is designed as a standalone Progressive Web App. You can install it on your Android phone, iPhone, iPad, Windows PC, or Mac desktop. Once cached by our Service Worker, core document manipulation tools are available offline anytime, anywhere.

4. Transparent & Completely Free

DocToolKit requires no account registrations, passwords, or credit card details. There are no artificial daily quotas, file count paywalls, or intrusive watermarks stamped onto your output documents.
