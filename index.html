Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions regarding DocToolKit's privacy, performance, and features.

Are my files uploaded to any server when using DocToolKit?
No. All file processing happens locally on your computer or phone using modern browser technologies (WebAssembly, HTML5 Canvas, and PDF.js). Your confidential PDFs, photos, and signatures never travel to our servers or any third-party cloud.
How does the continuous compression slider work?
The 1% to 100% slider controls genuine compression parameters. Higher percentages apply stronger downsampling and JPEG optimization curves to maximize size reduction, while lower percentages prioritize pristine visual fidelity.
Is DocToolKit compatible with mobile browsers?
Yes. DocToolKit is completely responsive across iOS Safari, Android Chrome, tablet viewports, and desktop monitors. You can also add it to your home screen as a Progressive Web App (PWA).
What file formats are supported?
DocToolKit supports standard PDF documents (.pdf) along with major raster image formats including JPEG (.jpg, .jpeg), PNG (.png), and WebP (.webp).
Is there a daily limit or paid subscription?
No. DocToolKit is completely free with no usage caps, watermarks, or account registration requirements.
